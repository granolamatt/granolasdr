CUDA THREAD HFChannelizer — Wideband FFT, Band Select & Audio gm/cuda/HFChannelizer.cc
Processing steps
  • Cast int16_t → float32, H2D copy
  • Overlap-save: copy tail to head of fftInData_d
  • R2C FFT: 1,400,000 points (cuFFT) → 700,001 bins, 0–70 MHz
  • 4 tunable audio sinks: 480-bin gather → batched C2C IFFT → 48 kHz ZMQ
  • Zero composite buffer (4096-pt)
  • Copy 11 HF band slices into composite buffer (2110 of 4096 bins)
  • Every 64 frames: SpectrumNorm equalization in-place on channelData_d
  • C2C IFFT: 4096 points → extract center 2048 samples
  • D2D async copy to output ring → MagBlock wakes

IN HFCHANNELIZER · EVERY 64 FRAMES SpectrumNorm — Noise Floor Equalization gm/cuda/SpectrumNorm.cu
Processing steps
  • D2H snapshot of 2,110 complex bins from fftData_d
  • Asymmetric EMA: α_down=0.10 (fast drop) · α_up=0.005 (slow rise, ~30 min τ)
  • 5-frame warm-up: α_down=0.30, α_up=0.10 to seed the estimate
  • Legendre polynomial fit (degree 3) on log10(EMA) per band
  • Compute per-bin gains to flatten intra-band tilt and RFI bumps
  • Cross-band leveling: geometric mean of 11 band-center EMAs
  • H2D gain upload → CUDA kernel multiplies channelData_d in-place
Parameters
EMA α_down (warm)0.10 — quick signal fade
EMA α_up (warm)0.005 — ~30 min rise τ
EMA α_down (warm-up)0.30 (5 frames)
EMA α_up (warm-up)0.10 (5 frames)
Poly fit degree3 (Legendre basis)
Levelinggeometric mean, 11 bands
Runs every64 frames ≈ 10 s

Result: all 11 bands appear at equal brightness in the waterfall regardless of natural propagation differences or band noise level.

complex<float> ring · 2,048 samples/block · 409.6 kHz · spectrally equalized
CUDA THREAD MagBlock<200> — Oversampled FFT & Magnitude Ring (Normal) gm/cuda/MagBlock.cc
Processing steps
  • Accumulate complex<float> blocks until window ≥ 2 × 65,536 samples (~320 ms)
  • 16 C2C FFTs: 4 time offsets × 4 freq offsets
    — time offset t: start at t × rfft_length/4
    — freq offset f: freqShift by f × bin_width/4 before FFT
  • magKernel: complex → dB-scaled uint8_t
  • D2D async copy → DeviceRingBuffer<uint8_t, 200> slot
  • Slide window by 65,536 samples → fire rate ≈ 6.25 Hz
Parameters
FFT size65,536 pts C2C
Bin resolution6.25 Hz/bin (1 FT8 tone)
Block duration160 ms (1 FT8 symbol)
Time OSR4 (offsets 0–3)
Freq OSR4 (shifts 0–3)
FFTs per block4 × 4 = 16
Ring slots200 blocks (~200 MB VRAM)
Block size in ring16 × 65,536 = 1,048,576 bytes

Fires at 6.25 Hz (one FT8 symbol period). FT8Cuda, JS8Cuda, and WaterfallCuda all hold const& to this ring — no second FFT needed.

CUDA THREAD FT8Cuda
gm/cuda/FT8Cuda.cc · FT8ScanCuda.cu
Processing steps
  • Wakes every 6 ring blocks (~960 ms)
  • Reads last 106 blocks (16.93 s) from DeviceRingBuffer
  • FT8ScanCuda: GPU Costas sync scan
    — max-log 8-FSK metric (or legacy freq+neighbor)
    — Costas {4,2,5,6,1,3,0} at 3 sync groups
    — score ≥ threshold → candidate list (fo, to, ts, fs)
  • FT8SoftCuda: soft symbols
    — 58 data syms × 8 mag bins → 3 LLRs/sym (Gray-coded 8-FSK)
    — output: log174 (174 floats/candidate)
  • D2H candidates + LLRs → decode callback
